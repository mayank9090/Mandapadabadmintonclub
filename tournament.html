Tournament Rules & Guidelines

Mandapada Badminton Club Official Rules, Regulations & Terms
₹

Entry Fee: ₹250 / Player (₹500 / Team)

Registration fee tournament shuru hone se pehle ya deadline ke andar coordinator ke paas jama karni anivary hai.

1 Tournament Format & Structure

  • Tournament strictly Doubles (2 vs 2) format me khela jayega.
  • Pehle sabhi registered teams ke beech Round Robin League matches honge.
  • League stage ke baad points aur net run/point difference ke aadhar par Top 4 Teams Playoffs (Semi-Finals) ke liye qualify karengi.
  • Playoffs ke winners aapas me Grand Championship Final khelenge.

2 Scoring & Points System

  • Matches standard badminton scoring rules ke mutabik Best of 3 Sets honge.
  • Har jeet (Win) par team ko 2 Points milenge aur haar (Loss) par 0 Points milenge.
  • Points table me tie hone par teams ka Net Point Difference dekha jayega.

3 Registration & Fees Policy

  • Har player ko निर्धारित Entry Fee (₹250 per player / ₹500 per team) pay karni hogi.
  • Fee non-refundable hai ek baar tournament schedule lock hone ke baad.
  • Team ka naam aur dono players ki jodi official roster me tabhi confirm hogi jab fee fully paid hogi.

4 Timing & Punctuality Guidelines

  • Sabhi players ko apne scheduled match time se 10 minute pehle court par report karna anivary hai.
  • Agar koi team scheduled time ke 10 minute baad bhi court par nahi pahunchti hai, toh opposing team ko Walkover (WO) de diya jayega.
  • Kharab mausam ya anya karan se schedule me badlav ki ghosana committee dwara ki jayegi.

5 Code of Conduct & Fair Play

  • Sabhi participants aur club members se sportsmanship aur apas me sauhard banane ki ummeed ki jati hai.
  • Referee ya umpire ka nirnay antim hoga. Kisi bhi tarah ka durvyavahar (Misbehavior) ya argument karne par team ko tournament se disqualify kiya ja sakta hai.
  • Court ke niyamo aur equipment ki dekhbhal karna har player ki zimmedari hai.

6 Trophies & Prizes Terms

  • Winner team ke liye Gold Trophy aur Runners-up ke liye Silver Trophy pradaan ki jayegi.
  • Finalists aur semi-finalists ko official club medals se sammanit kiya jayega.
  • Sabhi awards tournament ke closing ceremony me diye jayenge.
