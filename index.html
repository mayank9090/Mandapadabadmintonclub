Frequently Asked Questions

Mandapada Badminton Club se jude aam sawaal aur unke jawaab.

Hamare club me pure winter season (November se January) ki Seasonal Membership sirf ₹500 hai. Match khelne ke liye har 2 hafte me sirf ₹50 shuttle contribution dena hota hai.
Tournament me entry fee ₹250 per player (yani ₹500 per pair/team) hoti hai. Sabhi registered doubles teams hissa leti hain aur Round Robin League ke baad Top Teams Playoffs aur fir Grand Final khelti hain.
Club practice matches daily shaam ko floodlights ke under 7:00 PM se lekar 10:30 PM tak khele jaate hain. Tournament matches schedule ke hisab se hote hain.
Hamare arena me sabhi official matches strictly Doubles (2 vs 2) format me khele jaate hain taaki sabhi players ko barabar practice mile.
